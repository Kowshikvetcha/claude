# Behavioral — Map of Content

## Why this domain is asked
Every role in this vault ends with at least one behavioral round, and at 5 years' experience it's no longer a formality — panels expect a candidate to own project outcomes, defend real tradeoffs made under ambiguity, and negotiate an offer competently. It rarely disqualifies on its own, but a weak behavioral round routinely turns a "strong hire" technical performance into a "no hire."

## How it's tested per role
- **Data Scientist / ML Engineer / AI Engineer**: expect a dedicated behavioral round focused on project ownership, ambiguity handling and cross-team communication of technical tradeoffs.
- **MLOps Engineer**: behavioral questions often centre on incident handling and on-call ownership — "tell me about a production outage you fixed."
- **Agentic Engineer / FDE**: heavier weight on stakeholder and conflict questions, since these roles work closest to end clients and non-technical stakeholders.
- **All roles**: compensation negotiation and closing questions are asked near-universally in the final round regardless of track.
